# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Expected:** be respectful, welcome newcomers, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users. Persian and English are both welcome here.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, trolling, publishing others' private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributors that violate these rules. Report problems to **mahdi.mortazavi.135@gmail.com**; reports are handled confidentially.
